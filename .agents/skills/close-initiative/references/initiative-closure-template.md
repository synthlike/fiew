# Initiative closure proposal

## Destination and scope

State the original destination, success criteria, accepted scope changes, and the initiative map or parent artifact.

## Proposed outcome

Choose exactly one: **Achieved**, **Partially achieved**, or **Abandoned**. Explain the evidence-based rationale without presenting planned or deferred work as delivered.

## Outcome evidence

| Success criterion or commitment | Delivered behavior | Verification evidence | Limitation or gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Observable criterion | Current outcome | Test, review, demonstration, or operation | None or explicit gap |

## Delivered value

Summarize useful verified outcomes and link their specifications, decisions, implementation, and reviews.

## Gaps and limitations

List missing behavior, unavailable evidence, non-conformance, and operational limitations. Partial and abandoned outcomes must state these explicitly.

## Work reconciliation

| Work item | Current state | Evidence | Proposed disposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Linked issue or decision | Open, blocked, resolved, cancelled, or deferred | Relevant link | No longer needed, completed elsewhere, follow-up, or closure blocker |

## Proposed follow-up work

Draft only approved candidate outcomes and routing. Do not create issues from this proposal.

## Lessons and authority routing

Record lessons as supporting evidence. Identify any consequential decision, agreed behavior, resolved terminology, or executable work that belongs in an ARP, specification, domain model, or issue.

## Proposed artifact operations

List every map, parent, and issue update that would occur after approval.
