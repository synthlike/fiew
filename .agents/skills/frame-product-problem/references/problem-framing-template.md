# Product problem framing

## Decision and discovery boundary

State the product decision this brief informs, the founder or product-owner role consulted, and what remains outside current discovery.

## Proposed solution

Record the founder's proposed solution separately. Do not use solution features as the problem statement.

## Problem hypothesis

> When [actor] is in [context or trigger], they experience [problem], currently use [alternative], and seek [outcome]. We believe changing matters because [evidence or explicitly labeled assumption].

## Actors and audience segments

| Actor or segment | Role | Context and trigger | Desired outcome | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| User, buyer, beneficiary, or other actor | Relationship to problem and decision | Observable situation | Outcome, not feature | Source or unknown |

## Current behavior, alternatives, and workarounds

Describe what people do now, including doing nothing, and why each alternative may already be good enough. Link observations rather than inventing behavior.

## Claims and evidence

| Claim | Classification | Source and context | Confidence limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Material statement | Founder belief, direct observation, external evidence, interpretation, or unknown | Link or interview context | Missing or contradictory evidence |

## Counter-hypotheses

List credible alternative explanations, narrower audience hypotheses, and evidence that would distinguish them from the founder's framing.

## Risky assumptions and validation plan

| Assumption | Why it can invalidate the opportunity | Current state | Cheapest discriminating evidence | Strengthens | Weakens or contradicts | Inconclusive |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One testable assumption | Material consequence | Unexamined, Supported, Weakened, Contradicted, or Inconclusive | Ethical interview, observation, behavior, research, or prototype | Observable result | Observable result | Ambiguous result |

## Founder clarification still needed

List only unanswered questions that materially affect the framing. Ask them one at a time rather than treating this section as a questionnaire.

## Customer-validation guide

Link the separately approved non-leading questionnaire and session plan. State audience, consent and privacy boundaries, and how evidence will be retained.

## Evidence log and reassessment

Append dated evidence links and resulting assumption-state changes. Preserve prior reasoning and contradictory observations instead of rewriting history.

## Current recommendation

Choose continue, narrow, reframe, pivot, or stop. Explain the evidence and limitations. The founder makes the decision; do not claim validation from discussion alone.

## Next workflows

Link focused research, questionnaire, meetings, prototypes, initiative planning, decisions, domain work, or specifications without duplicating their authority.
