# Failure investigation: concise title

## Question and scope

State the unexpected behavior, affected boundary, and the next decision this investigation informs.

## Expected and observed behavior

- **Expected:** Behavior and authoritative source, or clearly marked unconfirmed expectation.
- **Observed:** Direct observation without causal interpretation.

## Environment and reproduction

Record relevant versions, configuration boundaries, input conditions, scope, frequency, and the smallest exact reproduction command or procedure. Redact secrets and unnecessary personal data.

## Evidence

List concrete code, configuration, test, log, trace, change, and external-source evidence. Include timestamps or correlation identifiers only when useful and safe.

## Hypotheses

| Hypothesis | Discriminating check | Evidence | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Plausible mechanism | Cheapest safe differentiator | Observation | Supported, weakened, falsified, or untested |

## Conclusion

State either:

- a supported root cause and the observed mechanism it explains; or
- bounded uncertainty, including what is known, what was falsified, what remains plausible, and what observation is missing.

## Probes and repository state

List disposable probes created, evidence obtained, cleanup performed, and anything explicitly approved for retention.

## Recommended next action

Recommend the smallest next action without presenting it as approved. Link any requesting issue or authoritative behavior.

## Verification

List commands and procedures actually run with their relevant results.
